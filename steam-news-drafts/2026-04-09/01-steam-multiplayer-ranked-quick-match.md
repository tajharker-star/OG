## Steam News Draft
We have pushed a major multiplayer update into Conquerors: Domination.

The game now supports a proper Steam-backed multiplayer flow instead of relying only on manual room coordination. Steam lobbies can now be created, advertised, discovered, joined, and invited through the integrated lobby path, while the new Ranked Quick Match queue gives players a dedicated 6-player free-for-all pipeline with auto-start rules and placement-based RP changes.

This update also tightens Steam lobby metadata, rich presence, and multiplayer stat syncing so hosted matches, ranked queues, and Steam-backed progression stay in sync much more reliably.

Highlights in this update:
- Steam multiplayer lobbies now support cleaner host, invite, and join flow.
- Ranked Quick Match is now a dedicated Steam queue with no bots.
- RP gain and loss now feed properly into long-term ranked progression.
- Matchmaking now prefers filling active ranked rooms before opening new ones.
