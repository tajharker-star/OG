## Steam News Draft
The tutorial sandbox has been pushed much closer to a real training ground.

Instead of dropping players into a vague practice space, the tutorial now makes the next step much clearer. The construction interface, tutorial objectives, and map-specific goals work together so players can understand what to build, where to place it, and which economy path they should stabilize before they commit to pressure.

This same pass also tightened local practice flow so players can add bots more reliably and learn match pacing without hidden friction.

Highlights in this update:
- Added stronger live tutorial guidance while practicing build order and expansion.
- Improved economy clarity around gold, oil, docks, and offshore production.
- Tightened local practice and bot-learning flow.
- Better support for learning before entering a full campaign or multiplayer match.
