## Steam News Draft
We have continued pushing progression and long-term tracking deeper into the lobby.

The stats and achievements screen now gives players a much better read on their long-term progress, including lifetime results, mode-specific records, ranked RP, and achievement completion. This work also connects directly to the newer onboarding flow, where clearing the tutorial and moving up through stronger bot difficulty milestones now feeds into the progression layer more cleanly.

This is part of a wider pass to make the demo feel less like isolated matches and more like a persistent strategy sandbox with readable progression.

Highlights in this update:
- Added stronger lifetime, mode-specific, co-op, and ranked stat visibility.
- Ranked RP progress is now easier to read from the lobby.
- Tutorial graduation and higher bot-difficulty milestones now fit into the achievement flow.
- Steam-ready achievement support remains aligned with the in-game achievement panel.
